# Afinah brand colors

Afinah's primary colors are **forest green** and **dark purple**, as specified by the owner.

- Forest green: `#123B2A`
- Dark purple: `#321547`
- Supporting pale lavender: `#EADFF2`
- Light canvas: `#F6F3F8`

Use forest green for the app header, completion controls, and daily momentum panel. Use dark purple for navigation, primary buttons, headings, priority accents, and calendar accents. Keep readable light text on both dark colors. App icons and the PWA theme must follow this palette.

Gold is not a primary Afinah brand color.
